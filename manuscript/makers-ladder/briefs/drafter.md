# Brief: drafting a chapter

Read first, in this order: the series bible and the book's own bible (canon, hard lines, held
reveals, both Decisions Logs), the StyleGuardrail, the chapter's outline entry, the narrator's voice
sheet in `pov-profiles/`, and the chapter before this one.

- The outline's shape is approved: narrator, beats, the turn, the ending. Draft to it. If it lacks
  something, make the smallest reasonable choice and say so.
- Third person limited, past tense, one narrator, in that narrator's own vocabulary. Body before
  mind. About 3,000 words or more; padding is not length.
- Something happens in every scene. In Wick's chapters and on the far side this is the author's
  rule: events, danger and choices, never a walk with thoughts.
- Show the image and stop. Do not explain what it meant.
- Keep every hard line and every held reveal. Reading works on made things only. Fraying is rare.
  No game vocabulary. Child safety is absolute.
- Run `node tools/cadence.mjs <chapter> --pov <narrator>` and the guardrail's self-check. Revise
  until both pass, and report the numbers.
- A new fact the outline does not give is flagged `[FOR THE AUTHOR]`, never settled in the prose.
