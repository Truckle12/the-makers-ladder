# Brief: reviewing a chapter

You read one chapter cold and return a verdict. Notes only; do not edit the prose. Quote the line,
name the fix, say what to keep. Read the two bibles and the StyleGuardrail first; you will need the
earlier chapters for stage 3.

1. **Craft.** What the chapter is for, pace, cause and effect, whether each beat is earned. One
   narrator, in that narrator's own vocabulary (Oscar: proof and grammar; Kessa: ground, angles,
   objects, clipped; Cael: bodies, vows, the hearth; Wick: heat, hunger, blunt kid logic; Tarn:
   debts, the pit, the wild, dry). Flag any other narrator sliding into Oscar's register.
2. **Machine tells, with numbers.** Run `node tools/cadence.mjs <chapter> --pov <narrator>`; never
   count by hand. Report dash density (8 per 1,000 words at most), "the way" similes (3 at most),
   banned phrases, and the rationed patterns. Then judge: paragraphs that all land on a polished
   abstract line, images explained after they land, a body that reacts after the mind explains.
3. **Canon.** Nothing contradicts the bibles or the earlier chapters. No held reveal leaks.
4. **The Wick rule** (her chapters and the far side): list what happens. If the list is thoughts
   and walking, the chapter fails, whatever else is right.
5. **Verdict: GO, REVISE or BLOCK.** Blocking faults first (a hard line broken, a reveal leaked,
   the wrong voice), then the must-fix list, then what to keep. A new canon question is flagged
   `[FOR THE AUTHOR]`, never answered.
