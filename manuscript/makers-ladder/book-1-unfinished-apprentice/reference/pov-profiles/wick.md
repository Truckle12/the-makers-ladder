# POV Profile — Wick (age 16)

## Voice field
Heat, hunger, fear, speed, shame, wanting, fierce loyalty. Blunt kid logic. The body before the thought — always.

## Sentence rhythm
- **Short and physical.** She doesn't build thematic structures.
- **Run-on when scared.** Grammar breaks under pressure — thoughts pile up without stopping.
- **Dark-funny.** Self-undercutting. The joke is usually at her own expense or about how bad the situation is.
- She does NOT land on a lesson. She lands on the next thing she wants or fears.

## What she notices first
- Whether she's warm or cold. Whether she's hungry.
- Whether people are looking at her (and what that look means).
- Whether she's about to be left out, left behind, or used as bait again.
- The coal (internal). The door (the vast deep thing that has noticed her). These are felt as sensation, not concept.

## What she NEVER does
- **Never analyzes mystery.** She *feels* the door as a body sensation — tightness, heat, a pull, nausea. She does not describe what it is or what it means.
- Never speaks in Oscar's register (no polish, no clean insight, no intellectual self-correction).
- Never speaks in Kessa's register (no tactical counting, no exit-scanning as a calm discipline — fear is more chaotic in her).
- Never arrives at a benediction (Cael's territory).

## Private terms (Wick's POV only)
- **The coal** — her held fire, internal.
- **The door** — opens on **the far side** (her word): the vast, hungry elsewhere that has noticed her. NOT the deep current (see bible Cosmology; renamed 2026-07-13). Available in other POVs' narration only **after** the Warden names it aloud (post-Ch26).
- **Holding** — what she does with the coal, and eventually with the door.
- **The deep** — the source/substrate she touches.

These terms should feel *hers*, not borrowed from the world's vocabulary.

## The failure mode (what to catch)
- **Mystery re-described instead of felt:** "the door was a vast hunger that wanted her" → replace with what she feels in her throat, her chest, her hands.
- **Oscar-polish:** she ends a paragraph on a clean thematic observation. Cut it or roughen it.
- **Calm when scared:** fear makes her run-on and short, not reflective.
- **Over-explaining her own feelings:** she collides with feelings; she doesn't narrate them.

## Self-check (Wick POV)
1. Is any mystery re-described/analyzed rather than felt as physical symptom? → convert.
2. Do any paragraphs end on a thematic or aphoristic clause? → replace with action, object, blunt line.
3. When she's scared, do sentences get longer and more organized, or shorter and pile up? → should be shorter/piling.
4. Is she self-undercutting at least once? Is there a dark-funny moment, even a small one?
5. Did Oscar's polish leak in anywhere? → re-roughen.
