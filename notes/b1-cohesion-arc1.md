# Cohesion read — Arc 1 (Ch1–10)

## A. EXPORTS

**Characters at arc end (Ch10, Marrow gate):**
- Oscar — knows he is a copy ("the man he had been copied from", Ch8), "a day old" at Ch9; hands still "new, unlined" but solid, frost gone; loop mark "only a scar" on the **back of the right hand** (Ch9); empty shoulder "ached" (Ch9). Magic: deep current "a film" → after the font "a true well… not the ocean" (Ch8); lance works again (Ch10); still has the Measure (body-cost speed) and the seeing-thread. Sword "at his hip" (Ch10). No eyebrow scar (the original had one, from a duel).
- Kessa — 40+, brown skin, pale eyes, cropped grey-shot hair; "worked this country thirty years" (Ch10). Crossbow. Carries a folding knife "not her knife" (Doss's, implied, Ch4); "knife out" Ch10. Uninjured.
- Cael — thirties, nose broken twice, "broad scarred hand"; long-hafted hammer; iron **hearth-mark token** at throat (small hill-and-hearth god); grace costs him grey fatigue. Forearm gash (Ch3) grace-knit (Ch4), never mentioned after.
- Wick — "sixteen at the most", dark braid, reddish branching birthmark below the ear that glows near power; wild-touched heat in hands. Now aims ("I aimed", Ch10); "hummed… instead of crackling" (Ch9). Uninjured.
- Doss — dead before Ch1; "the one who said no"; held a vault door in "the last place".
- Quill — a bird (talons, feathers, dry rasp), absent; stirs "at the edge of the world" Ch8, "closer than the night before" Ch9.
- The Redactor — faceless corrector, cut loose from the city's text and swept into the collapse; **not dead**: "loose in the world now, blind to the line it followed… A lost thread can be found" (Ch9).

## C. FLAGS

1. **SEAM** — Ch3 "the last of Kessa's bolts" spent in the street; then Ch6 "Kessa's bolt took it in the chest", Ch8 "Each bolt struck… one bolt at a time", Ch10 "Kessa's bow spoke twice." No recovery or resupply line.
2. **SEAM** — Ch5 "the same word the water had handed him by the fire" (*unfinished*). Ch4's water scene gives "new" and "*Made.*"; "unfinished" never occurs there.
3. **SEAM, near BREAK** — Ch7 "the way she'd reached for the font in the—the way she reached for any bright thing." Wick has never reached for a font before; "the font in the—" has no antecedent and reads as an unfinished edit, not a stumble.
4. **SEAM** — Loop location drifts: Ch1 "in the web of skin between his own thumb and forefinger", Ch2 "healed into the web of it"; Ch8 "on the back of his right hand the closed loop… blazed", Ch9 "the loop on the back of the right one." Right hand is constant; web vs back is not.
5. **SEAM** — Ch8 "the mark of all the Alberich line": the name appears once, cold, in narration; nobody has said or thought it; next occurrence is Ch28.
6. **SEAM** — Ch9 Cael "the nearest hold's two days' walk if the passes are open" vs Ch10 six days plus "two days on" to Marrow and "two hundred miles of nothing in every other direction." A nearer hold was skipped without comment, or the estimate is wrong.
7. **SEAM (POV lock)** — Ch9 last paragraph is omniscient: "though none of the four climbing down away from it knew yet to count them… Not one of those sleepers had a crew." Every other close in the arc is anchored to Oscar's senses.
8. **NIT** — Ch8 "the stair behind us is already gone": the only stair is the one the Redactor is at that moment descending.
9. **NIT** — Ch7 "the high road Kessa promised": in Ch3 "The high road's under all of that"; she promised the road that "goes down before it goes up."
10. **NIT** — Ch5 "You said you'd never been here": Oscar never says it on the page (Ch2 gives only "A traveler… trying to find his way home").
11. **NIT** — Ch3 "the way Cael had wanted to be approached an hour ago on the balcony": Kessa gave "Keep them up"; Cael arrived after.
12. **NIT** — Ch3 "three hundred years of cracked masonry": the city is "longer than the maps go back"; three hundred is Oscar's number.
13. **NIT** — Ch2 Kessa "wheel-mark on the guard" vs Ch1/Ch10 "below the guard."
14. **NIT** — Ch10 drift-cats "Six, maybe seven"; on-page kills are four (Cael, Kessa, Oscar, Wick "the last of them").
15. **NIT** — Ch7 paragraph "And he wanted it" follows Cael's speech; "he" reads as Cael for a beat.
16. **NIT** — Ch9 "Oscar took the last watch… he slept," then "He sat his watch": he sleeps through the watch he took.

Not flagged (deliberate doubles): "Right… There it is." Ch2/Ch7; "did not sleep much and slept badly" Ch4/Ch9; the fist-sized-place lesson Ch3/Ch8/Ch10.

## D. Arc verdict

Chapters 1–10 read as one continuous story with one set of facts: the crew, the wounds, the magic ledger (film → candle → well), the Doss backstory and the teaching thread all carry cleanly, and every chapter opens on the previous close. The weakest join is the Ch8→Ch10 seam where the book leaves the dead city — the loop mark migrates from thumb-web to hand-back, "Alberich" drops without setup, Cael's "two days" becomes eight, and Kessa's spent quiver silently refills — small facts a fold or compression pass appears to have carried over without their sources. Inside the city the one real stumble is the half-sentence in Ch7 ("the font in the—"), which a reader will hit and reread.
