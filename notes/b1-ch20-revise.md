1. **L5** — "That was the whole of it. That was the thing the pit had nearly killed her to learn and the square had let her prove."
   → "That was the whole of it: **the** thing the pit had nearly killed her to learn, and the square had let her prove."
   *Reason:* collapse back-to-back "That was the —" pivots; colon (not a new dash) keeps em-count flat.

   → "...patient and *interested,* **the same as it always was now since the pit,** like a thing that had gotten a good look at her..."

3. **L21** — "she felt, faintly, **the way you feel** a word you can't remember—"
