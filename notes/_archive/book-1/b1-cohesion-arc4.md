## C. FLAGS

1. **SEAM** · Ch40 · "*I told him... I said I can't be a person and a hole at the same time. Cael thought I was being brave.*" — no such exchange in the book. A memory of a line never said.
2. **SEAM** · Ch41 · Cael: "She was standing right there... she looked at me and said it was all right. To *me.*" — Ch40 (Wick's POV) has her pass "Cael's hip" wordless; she speaks only to Oscar, then counts from the chair.
3. **SEAM→BREAK** · Ch39/40 → Ch41 · Struck out: "the nearest of the lamplit bridges... Then a garden. Then a whole pale street," with "one man with a bucket"; erasure leaves "no gap... just a piece of the world that had never held one." Ch41: "the lamps were still burning on the bridges"; Kessa finds "The street was exactly a street," "a bucket standing in a doorway," and does not knock. The erased came back and nobody registers it; Oscar's "Nothing's broken. There's nothing *left*" doesn't cover restoration. One beat of Kessa noticing closes it.
4. **SEAM** · Ch35 · "She didn't say so. She'd stopped saying so somewhere south of the waystation." vs Ch34's order ("you say it. Every time") and Ch36 "about forty times since the waystation."
5. **SEAM** · the Fair House clock · Ch37 enters at "full light" (market "an hour," then the climb); Ch38 "Whatever stood at this basin *tonight*"; Ch39 "an hour"; Ch40 "a quarter of an hour"; Ch41 "in the evening," basin "an hour before," Cael "woke a little before dark... no use for an hour," then out "at last light." 6. **SEAM** · doubled wound · Ch33 "laid his forearm open near to the bone. He changed hands" / Ch39 "opened Tarn's forearm to the bone... held the line with the other arm." Same wound, beat and words (Ch25/26 already did it twice).
7. **SEAM** (POV lock) · Ch35, Oscar POV · "It was leaning on her door as it had leaned at the spring, patient, glad, sure of its welcome." Oscar's looking gives "nothing on it, and never has" (Ch36); "glad, sure of its welcome" is Wick's interior (Ch40/42).

9. **NIT** · Ch39 · Kessa "shot the Setter... already re-cocking." The crossbow (Ch1–27) is unseen for twelve chapters — she throws Doss's knife at Corwen, the Ch34 inventory ignores the wage-men's "two crossbows" — and after one bolt it vanishes again.

11. **NIT** · Ch41 "I've spent this entire year running away from it" vs "alive for one season" (Ch38, 39); Ch42 "in her fist for months" vs Marrow "three weeks"/"last month and more" (Ch35).

## D. Arc verdict

The arc reads as one story with one set of facts: every item, count, gauge number and wound tracks from Ch33 through Ch42, each chapter opens on the last one's close, and the rebuilt Ch42 agrees with Ch40/41 on every fact checked. The weakest join is Ch40→Ch41: two remembered lines (Wick's "person and a hole," Cael's "she said it was all right") point at scenes that don't exist, and the erased street with the man and his bucket is back the next morning without anyone saying so. Fix those three and the Fair House clock (morning in, "tonight" at the basin, evening out), and the arc is whole.
