## C. FLAGS

1. **SEAM** Ch18: "He filed the feeling away with the slanting hand in the grimoire and the loop on the southern coin" — no southern coin bearing the loop has been seen; Ch19 stages it as a fresh shock ("looking at a coin in a fruit-seller's palm, at the loop on it… *That's my mark on their money*").
2. **SEAM** Ch19: "'He's not chasing us,' Oscar said, for the second time in two days" — there is no first time; the phrase occurs once in the arc.
3. **SEAM** Ch19: Wick "Then why duck the cord at all?" → Oscar "The cordon isn't his; it's theirs… Every patrol we slip… The paper has a line for unregistered wild-touched" — no southern cord/cordon/patrols exist before this exchange; "wild-touched" appears exactly once, unexplained; "the paper" is unidentified (not Corwen's note; Oscar never saw the finders' board).
4. **SEAM** Ch21: "Brida said my gift was a noose down here. I thought I understood her." — Brida never said it; it is Oscar's own thought in Ch19 ("His gift… was a noose down here"), and "I thought I understood her" is already attached in Ch19 to Brida's real line "the kinder it gets, the closer it is".
5. **SEAM** Ch16/17 day-count: Wick, day of surfacing: "You dragged me out of that hole *today*." Next noon Oscar: "two days ago, walked down into the good claim… we killed it. Two days ago." Sarn's "crew that went down this morning" only works if the kill was the day before Ch16 — then Wick's "today" is wrong; otherwise Oscar's "two days" is.
6. **SEAM** Ch16: "We dragged you out of that font" (Wick) and, five lines on, "You stood at that font and you didn't go in" (Cael) — two versions of the same event.
7. **SEAM** Ch20: "where two days ago the figure had come apart in his fingers" — Ch18 sets both failures ("tried, twice, on the road") on or before the tree night, ≥7 days earlier; Ch20 itself says "failed this figure twice on the open road". No third attempt exists.
8. **SEAM** Ch19: "He'd nearly died reading the working in her lamp" — Ch11 Oscar is explicit: "That's not the debt and it's not the lamp. That paper's about me."
9. **SEAM** Ch20 (Cull): Cael's hearthward — "stops a blow, stops a fall of stone" (Ch14), on his arm through Ch17 — is never raised, mentioned, or missed in the battery run, and is absent from Ch18–21 entirely. Oscar's brace saves a man who is carrying a warding shield.

## D. Arc verdict

The arc reads as one continuous story with one set of facts: the loop, the writ, the grimoire hand, Quill's pull, Wick's door and token, and Brida's warnings all carry forward without drift, and the Ch19→20→21 joins (Hollet's Cross → Cull → Oressa) are the cleanest in the book so far. The weakest join is Ch18→19, where Ch18 pre-empts Ch19's coin discovery and Ch19 twice answers lines that were never spoken ("second time in two days"; the cord/cordon/"wild-touched" exchange), followed by the Ch16/17 "today"/"two days ago" clock and the shield that silently leaves Cael's arm after the square. Nothing here would stop a reader, but Flags 1–4 would each catch a careful one.
