## Ch41 — Set Aside — **7 / ON VOICE**

Inward, but inward her way: counts, hands, stones, a bucket, a rat. "She looked at her hands. They were fine. Nothing had touched her all day." The pass cut every aphorism it aimed at — and nicked the sentences on either side.

**Leaks**
- "There was nothing to say to him that wouldn't have been a kind of collecting." — abstract landing. → cut; "She did not knock." then "She went back and got her people." already follows.
- "some clever careful hopeful structure" — Oscar's mind in Oscar's nouns. → "heard his voice go up the way it did before a long answer, and heard him stop it."
- "lay there in the evening being beautiful" — ornament. → "lay there in the evening and did not know."
- "A thing held up by a working ought to come down when the working goes." — general truth; tolerable, cuttable.

**Damage**
- "she looked away and let him have it." — with the theft clause cut, this now reads as the idiom (she rounds on him) at the bread beat. → "and left him to it."
- "They went out through the west gate at dawn, and split at the milestone. Nobody had wanted a gate for it." — they just walked through a gate; reads as contradiction. → "Not under the arch. Nobody had wanted that for it."
- Cael's grief now stammers into parody: "She said that. … she said it was all right. To me. She said it to *me.*" and "Every time I knew. Every single time, I knew … I don't know this time. … and I don't know." → one restatement each: "She told me it was all right. Sixteen, going out onto that stone, and she looked at me and said it. To me." / "Every time, I knew which thing I was standing over. Not this time."
- "She had never yet failed to find a way into somewhere. Some she hadn't liked." — *Some* has no referent. → "Some of the ways in she hadn't liked."
- "She stood there a while. … and Kessa stood there and tried to make the man with the bucket count" — doubled. → drop the second "stood there and".
- "and his home behind him," — the qualifier that explained it ("unfinished, refusing to be finished" — the title echo) is gone; bare, it reads as if Everset is simply his home. Either cut the phrase or make it plain: "the city that had made him behind him".

**Ends on a thing she sees/does?** Yes — the rock riding against her ribs.
