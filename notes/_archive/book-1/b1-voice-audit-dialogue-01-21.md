# Dialogue voice audit — Book 1, Ch 1–21 (spoken lines only)

Read: the five POV profiles, the guardrail's voice/dialogue sections, bible §Characters, then Ch1–21 in order. MUST = a non-Oscar character unmistakably speaks Oscar's register; SHOULD = a polished/aphoristic landing to roughen; COULD = defensible quip that adds to sameness. Tarn is absent (joins Ch23); Doss never speaks on-page.

## Closing summary
**Most drift (top 5):** **Ch13** (Kessa's ghost-writ monologue, two Cael epigrams, Oscar's arithmetic rally); **Ch19** (Cael's courtesy epigram, four Kessa landings, the orphaned "Which is?", the duplicated bowl beat); **Ch15** (Cael's benediction, the mid-fight quip trio, Wick's strategy speech, Oscar's post-pit speech); **Ch16** (Wick's arithmetic speech, Cael's "cleaner sum", Oscar's second Tithe speech); **Ch20** (Oscar's three-epigram mentor speech, Kessa's writing metaphor).

**Most common pattern:** the *sentence-final epigram* — a non-Oscar character ends a speech on a general truth ("A man who's certain doesn't hurry", "a person is mostly what they do", "his whole horror is the courtesy", "You just get better at being tired", "That's the trouble with a road"). About two-thirds of flags are this shape; the fix is identical each time: delete the last clause, end on the fact. Second: the *ensemble button* — Ch15 fight and Ch17 steps each have three characters landing a quip in sequence; keep one per scene.

**Instantly tellable?** Yes when short: Kessa on terrain, Cael on bodies, Wick on wanting. No whenever any of them is handed the scene's summary — from Ch13 on, the summarizer's voice is Oscar's regardless of speaker.

**Fix scope:** ~44 lines, 6 MUST (Kessa Ch13 "money and mystery"; Ch19 "Which is?" bug and duplicate bowl beat; Cael Ch15 benediction and Ch19 courtesy line; Wick Ch16 speech). Nearly all are trims — cut the final clause — not rewrites; no scene needs restructuring. One dialogue-only pass over Ch13, 15, 16, 19, 20, with spot-fixes in Ch4, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 17, 18, 21. For Oscar: consolidate the Tithe speech to the square, retire "arithmetic" from his mouth so it stays the Tithe's word, and let one Ch20 line misfire.
