## 3. [FOR THE AUTHOR] — major changes held for shape approval

### A. (BLOCKING canon) Oscar reads a *living* tree (L71–73) — *needs shape approval*
The night-fire study scene ends with Oscar turning his **looking** on the living tree and *succeeding*: "the tree resolved for him not as bark and bud but as *workmanship*… he could **read** it now, clean and deep and fast." This directly contradicts the **locked HARD SCOPE**: reading is **made-only**; "on the living and the wild his gift returns **nothing**" (bible §World/Setting & Rules; Continuity Watch-Outs: "Never let it read … animals, or nature. The living/wild returns nothing"). It also contradicts the prior-chapter establishment in **Ch25**, where reading the living deep is "the worst idea I've ever had," an urge Oscar *holds back*, and where he reads only "the deep current itself — the raw written stuff that ran under everything **made**" (the Script surfacing), never the living tissue.

I did **not** edit this, because the clean fix isn't surgical — it *inverts* a thematic growth beat ("my gift is sharpening on the living world") into its opposite, and that touches both canon and the scene's meaning. Two canon-aligned directions for the author to choose:
- **(preferred) The tree returns nothing.** His gift goes silent on the living — and that blindness is the point. It foreshadows the Arc-3 lesson (his reading is the *wrong tool* for the living rot / the grey) and deepens "a made man cannot read the unmade world." Stronger thematically than a generic "I'm getting better" beat.
- **(Ch25-consistent) Scope it to the Script.** He reads only the *deep current / written substrate* the tree is written *on*, explicitly not the living biology — mirroring the Ch25 spring fix. Lighter, but keeps a "getting better" note while staying canon-clean.

Either way, the two remaining "the way" tics inside this paragraph will dissolve in the rewrite, so I left them.
