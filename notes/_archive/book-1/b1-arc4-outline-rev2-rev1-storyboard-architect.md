## 2. What PR #38 changed (binding — these override the task brief and the bible)

The task brief told me to keep the font substrate **undecided** and to keep Corwen's death at ~Ch41.
PR #38 decides the first and moves the second. Per the run rules ("where feedback conflicts with the
task, the feedback wins"), I applied all four:

1. **Font = arcane / the deep current** (author's words: *"the font should be arcane magic, the same
   type oscar uses — the deep current"*). → **Resolved** the rev1 Tier-0 contradiction. rev2 no longer
   sketches "two branches"; it commits to the arcane reading (Oscar can partly *read* the font) and
   flags canon-keeper to reconcile the bible's "apex **living** magic" wording. Consequence: Wick's
   "door onto the deep" is the **same** current accessed raw — a clean, single-substrate cosmology that
   the bible's own "reality is written on the deep current" line already supports.
2. **Corwen is not the big-bad; kill him earlier, by Cael, as the Ch34 cost** (*"he could be killed
   earlier by Cael and thats the cost we talked about elsewhere in caels chapter… Corwen is a hunter
   but thats just a pawn in a larger game"*). → Relocated Corwen's death from ~Ch41 to **Ch34**, made
   the **killing itself Cael's cost** (a mercy-sworn man forced to kill — his one POV chapter now
   carries real moral weight), and opened a **new-villain slot** for the finale. Overturns the
   2026-06-29 lock → canon-keeper.
3. **De-center Oscar's reading/unmaking; shrink "the sweep"** (*"id prefer it to remain just a useful
   ability… it should [not] be the only focal point"*; and *"i honestly don't know what the sweep
   is"*). → Demoted "the sweep" to a modest, wide-but-shallow battlefield tool (or optionally cut it),
   explicitly **not** the mechanism that wins the climax. The finale is won by the **ensemble + Wick's
   sacrifice.** Reading gets the crew *to* the crux and is then *not enough* — a deliberate handoff to
   Wick.
4. **Shape 1 ratified** (*"yes, go with shape 1"*). → Marked Shape 1 as author-confirmed; kept Shapes
   2/3 only as a record of why 1 won.

Plus the author's steer for the back half: *"from the hand off to the end i think we should be building
tension on who the real villian is."* → rev2's back-half tension engine is the **real-villain reveal**
(seeded Ch29/34, dread Ch35–37, present Ch38, fused with the Everset=Evermere reveal Ch39).

### The new villain — what I proposed (and why it's flagged, not decided)

PR #38 asks me to *"prove [Corwen big enough] or find a new villain… tied to Corwen and everset."* I
took the second path and proposed three candidates in a dedicated **§New Villain** section, recommending
**"the Setter"**: the apparatus/operator that performs the **Setting** at the font's seat — the thing
Oscar was *made to be finished by.* Rationale: deepest canon resonance (Arc 1's climax was literally
"Setting"; Oscar's premise is being "set"), it's a **Redactor-analog operator** so revealing it does
**not** reveal the architect (Book-2 hold), and it ties cleanly to both Corwen (his master; his errand
was fetching the copy home to be set) and Everset (it *is* the font's will). Alternatives offered: a
House hierophant (legible, smaller charge) and a prior "set" copy (chilling mirror, but risks the
fair-copy Book-2 hold). Left as `[FOR THE AUTHOR]` #1 — it's the top ratification the back half needs.
