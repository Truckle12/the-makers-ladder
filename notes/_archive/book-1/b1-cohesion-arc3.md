## C. FLAGS

1. **SEAM · 23** "the gesture he'd used in the cell, the one that meant *owing*" — no such gesture in Ch22's cell scene.
2. **SEAM · 28** "eight down and three still on their feet" — eight is the whole ambush; five are down by the page's own tally. Drift: 29 "four men still standing"; 30 "six House men bleeding on it".
3. **SEAM · 28** "the man Oscar had put in the marsh" — Kessa's scree man reached the marsh; Oscar's went "three yards down the scree".
4. **SEAM · 28** "You said washing's for people who think the dirt's the problem" — Oscar never says this anywhere in the manuscript.
5. **SEAM · 29→31** Captivity is three days by Ch29 ("Two days she gridded… On the third morning"; "hunted for three days"; "The lane's been down since yesterday noon") but "two days" throughout 30–31 ("Two days he'd been her guest"). Also 29 Tarn on the third morning: "Two days ago the country funneled us"; 31 Tarn "had not mentioned the arm once in three days" (Oscar has had him one night).
6. **SEAM · 30** Ash: "The rest he gets as we go" and Wick: "She's taking us to the place that made you" vs Ash, same scene: "I'll be gone by the time you're done arguing about me in the morning" / "I don't walk it with him".
7. **SEAM · 32** one chapter, four clocks: "Five days ago, by the map"; "Two days now she'd watched it work" (the owl has been with them since day one); "in four days the girl had nodded"; "Tarn found the waypost on the fourth day".
8. **SEAM · 32** "her people, four and her, five with the bird" — four and her is already five.
9. **SEAM · 26 POV** Kessa's interiority in an Oscar chapter: "A trail she'd have sworn was an hour cold ran colder… the land lied about which way was down"; Tarn's: "Tarn felt it before any of them saw it… hunting the deep in his blood".
10. **NIT · 26** "For two days he'd guided them" — three by Ch24 and by the same chapter's "For three nights the old trees had glowed".
11. **NIT · 22** "He had watched it from the box an hour ago, in the earlier bout" — earlier bouts were watched from the stands ("an hour ago in the stands").
12. **NIT · 23** "last night I beat a man the size of a barn. Tonight…" / "You burned too close last night" — same night, pre-dawn.
13. **NIT · 28** "Weeks off the map" / "First time in weeks" — ~9–10 days by the arc's own count.
14. **NIT · token** Named once (22), pocket-hand (23), then silent nine chapters including the grey; 22 also reads Tarn's recognition off "the hand curled tight in her pocket… one carrier of a hungry, hooked thing, clocking another", fusing token with fire.
15. **NIT · 26** "since the pit" — in this arc "the pit" has meant the Oressa bowl ("forty years of the pit").
16. **NIT · 32** "glassed the way ahead" — spyglass with no prior existence.
17. **NIT · Warden** "Come back if you can" (26) never answered; the toll closes off-page; edge-stone setup unpaid in arc.
18. **NIT · Tarn's arm** forearm wounds (25/26) never sided and vanish once the shoulder takes over; 30 "ate with his knife hand and kept the strapped arm in his lap" makes his knife hand the left.

## D. Arc verdict

The arc reads as one continuous story with one set of facts: items, wounds, the count and the who-knows-what ledger carry cleanly, and the big transfers (Tarn in, Oscar taken and retaken, Quill given) are each set up and paid. The weakest join is the manse (Ch29→30/31), where the calendar flips from three days to two, Ash promises to travel with them and then leaves, and the ambush arithmetic that feeds Kessa's read of Corwen doesn't add. Everything else is a nit a copy-edit fixes in a line.
