## Diagnosis (before edits)

The prior revision landed well; the prose is strong. The one systemic drift was **Oscar-polish leaking into Wick's interiority** — a 16-year-old whose profile is "blunt kid logic, short and physical, run-on when scared, mystery felt not analyzed" was narrating in balanced, interpretive, thematically-resolved clauses. Two concrete patterns:

1. **The door/deep re-described, not felt.** The same idea — *the thing on the far side of the door had noticed/wanted her and never once stopped* — is stated three times (¶ at old lines 9, 45, 67) as intent-attribution ("patient and *interested,*" "like a thing that had gotten a good look at her and decided to wait," "hadn't stopped noticing since"). This is exactly the profile's flagged failure mode ("the door was a vast hunger that wanted her → replace with what she feels in her throat, chest, hands"). It's also the guardrail's "mystery via symptoms, not re-description."
2. **Aphoristic paragraph landings.** Several paragraphs resolve on a tidy thematic clause a narrator would write, not a beat Wick would land on ("It was restful, having something in her life that wasn't trying to be more than it was"; "…being good at a thing didn't mean doing it completely by yourself"; "…some things a person held alone on purpose"). Profile: she does NOT land on the lesson; she lands on the next thing she wants or fears.
3. **Calm-when-scared.** The stream-aftermath paragraph (old line 61) was a long, balanced, elegantly-repeated sentence about fear — the opposite of the profile's "grammar breaks under pressure; thoughts pile up."

4-stage check otherwise clean: **Editor** — structure/pacing fine, no changes needed. **Consistency** — no contradictions with bible; Wick's private terms (coal/door/holding/the deep) stay in her POV; "door" is fine here (her own POV). **Hard lines** — clear (no pet-names, no minor-sexualization, no game vocab, no reading of living/minds, fraying not invoked, reveal discipline intact — the deep/door stay unnamed-in-mechanism).

## Change log (before → after + reason)

**(c) Re-description → bodily symptom — 2 conversions**

1. **¶ "She'd held the whole battle…"** (old line 9)
   - Before: "…how the deep had been right there the entire time, patient and *interested,* the same as it always was now since the pit, like a thing that had gotten a good look at her and decided to wait."
   - After: "…how the whole time there'd been a cold pull down under the coal, low and steady, tugging at her, and it hadn't let up once since the pit. It didn't let up now. She'd stopped waiting for it to."
   - Reason: Symptom (cold pull, tugging, low) in her heat/cold field instead of attributing patience/intent to the deep. Lands on a blunt line. See FLAG below.

2. **¶ "He didn't know how close…"** (old line 45)
   - Before: "…but he didn't know about the *door,* or the thing on the far side of it that had noticed her down there in the dark and hadn't stopped noticing since."
   - After: "…but he didn't know about the *door.* He didn't know that since the pit there'd been a cold spot low at the back of her that no fire touched—not the coal, not the campfire, not the sun on her neck all afternoon."
   - Reason: Same fix — dread carried by a physical symptom (a cold spot no heat reaches) in her sensory register; lands on the sun-on-neck sensation (callback to ¶3). Also removes the 2nd of three near-identical "noticed/wanted her and never stopped" restatements; the climactic 3rd (old line 67) now stands alone and hits harder.

**(b) Aphoristic landing → action / object / blunt line — 3 fixes**

3. **¶ "She liked it because it was quiet…"** (old line 23)
   - Before: "…never once tried to take her over. It was restful, having something in her life that wasn't trying to be more than it was."
   - After: "…never once trying to take her over. One thing in her whole life that wasn't trying to get bigger. She put her thumb on it and pressed."
   - Reason: Lands on an action (thumb on the token), and "being a rock" earlier adds a small self-undercutting beat that sets up Kessa's "You're rubbing that rock again." Kid-blunt, not interpreted.

4. **¶ "She hadn't. She didn't, much…"** (old line 33)
   - Before: "…because Kessa carried her own dead in a knife she never put down and understood, better than anyone, that some things a person held alone on purpose."
   - After: "…Kessa carried her own dead in a knife she never put down; she wasn't the type to go digging in anybody else's pockets."
   - Reason: Cuts the thematic tail ("some things a person held alone on purpose") and lands on the object (the knife) + a plain, faintly dark-funny image in Wick's register.

5. **¶ "She was good at holding…"** (old line 51)
   - Before: "She just wished, sometimes, that being good at a thing didn't mean doing it completely by yourself."
   - After: "And she was so tired of doing it by herself."
   - Reason: Replaces a balanced aphorism with a blunt statement of exhaustion — a feeling, not a lesson. Keeps the alone-thread that feeds the ending.

**(a) Re-roughen toward run-on/pile-up when scared — 1 fix**

6. **¶ "She walked the rest of that day…"** (old line 61)
   - Before: "…wet and quiet and frightened in a way she couldn't point at, frightened of nothing in the world but herself, and by the time they made camp she was so tired of being the only one who knew it that she'd nearly stopped being able to bear it."
   - After: "…wet and quiet and scared, and the stupid part was she couldn't even say of what—not the road, not the cord, nothing out there she could hit. Just her. Just the thing she was. And by the time they made camp she was so sick of being the only one who knew it that she'd nearly stopped being able to carry it."
   - Reason: Breaks the balanced sentence into pile-up + fragments ("Just her. Just the thing she was."), adds a self-undercutting aside ("the stupid part"), and keeps her fear physical/blunt. Ends on "carry it," tying to the weight motif.

## [FOR THE AUTHOR] — roughened but the original had X

- **Old line 9:** "patient and *interested,* … like a thing that had gotten a good look at her and decided to wait." The symptom version is more in-voice, but the original had a genuinely eerie *characterization* of the deep as attentive/waiting that the symptom loses. If you'd rather keep the creepiness, a hybrid could keep one clause of felt-attention ("and something down there was awake to it") without returning to full re-description. Applied the fuller symptom version per this pass's direction (c); flagging in case the eeriness is load-bearing for the door's menace.

- **Ending (final two ¶, old lines 77 & 79) — left as-is (deliberate hold, not applied).** This is the chapter's most Oscar-polished spot: it lands on a stacked *double* aphorism ("she understood at last that holding a thing alone had never once made it lighter." / "It had only ever made her sure she was the only one strong enough to carry it."). Per the profile (§ she doesn't land on a lesson) and the guardrail (§ "keep the best line, cut the echo"), the cleanest touch would be to end the penultimate ¶ on the physical beat (coal gone "quiet and warm and *small*") and keep only the sharper, more characterful of the two closing lines (the "only one strong enough" truth). **I did not apply this** because (i) it's the earned emotional climax the prior revision built and was reported to "land well," and (ii) it edges past "surgical." Offered as an optional future touch, not a defect.
