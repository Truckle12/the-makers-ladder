Targeted voice pass on Ch20 for Wick's interiority only. The prior revision landed well — this pass has one job: roughen Wick's POV toward how she actually thinks and feels, not how a polished narrator would interpret her.

## Direction (binding)

(a) **Re-roughen toward blunt kid logic and run-on-when-scared.** Wick doesn't organize her thoughts into clean thematic clauses — she collides with them. When she's scared or confused, her sentences run or fragment; she doesn't land on the lesson.

(b) **Replace 2–3 aphoristic paragraph landings** — the ones that end on a tidy thematic observation — with action, object, or a blunt single line. Let the beat land on something physical or abrupt, not interpreted.

(c) **Convert 1–2 door/deep/token re-descriptions into bodily symptom.** She doesn't need to name or analyze what the door *is* — show it as something she feels in her chest, her hands, the back of her throat. Mystery via symptoms, not re-description.

## Constraints

- This touches most paragraphs. Protect genuinely strong lines — if a sentence already reads like Wick, leave it. Do not sand every peak flat chasing uniformity.
- Flag any line where the roughening risks losing something good: `[FOR THE AUTHOR] — roughened but the original had X`.
- No structural changes. Voice only.
